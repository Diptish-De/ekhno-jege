## **Figma AI Prompt — “এখনও জেগে” (Still Awake) | Immersive Hindi Music Website**

Design a **desktop-first, cinematic, immersive music-listening website** called **“এখনও জেগে” (Still Awake).**

The website should feel like a **room**, not an application.

The user should feel as if they are secretly sitting inside someone else's apartment at **3:42 AM**, listening to music while watching a young founder continue working through another sleepless night.

The room itself is the interface.

Do **not** create a traditional music application.

Avoid anything that resembles Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, or JioSaavn.

The illustration should remain visible at all times and should occupy **80–85% of the visual hierarchy.**

The UI should occupy only **15–20%** of the screen.

The design philosophy should be:

> Less interface. More atmosphere.

---

# Canvas

* Desktop layout
* 1920×1080 canvas
* 16:9 aspect ratio
* Full-screen hero section
* No scrolling on the landing page

---

# Visual Inspiration

Follow the visual language of:

* Boithak
* Bus Driver
* Truck Wala
* Panwadi
* Saloon
* Naacho

Use:

* Hand-painted textures
* Matte illustrations
* Vintage Bengali poster aesthetics
* Cinematic composition
* Soft brush strokes
* Layered shadows

Avoid:

* Minimalism
* Flat design
* Futuristic cyberpunk
* RGB lighting
* Neon effects

---

# Preserve the Existing Illustration

Do **not** modify the artwork.

Keep all existing elements:

* The old Kolkata apartment
* The rain
* The window
* The founder sitting at the desk
* The startup whiteboard
* The August 2026 calendar
* The Spider-Man poster
* The ceiling fan
* The books
* The tea stall outside
* The yellow taxi
* The handwritten notes
* The headphones
* The chai cup

The illustration is already complete.

The UI must be designed **around** it.

---

# Typography

Place the title in the empty upper-left area.

Use the provided Bengali typography asset.

The title should look hand-painted.

Do not place it inside a container.

Use:

```text
এখনও জেগে
```

Subtitle:

```text
Late-night Hindi songs for people who are still trying.
```

Keep the typography large.

Leave generous spacing.

Do not place any buttons directly beneath it.

---

# Navigation

Avoid a traditional navigation bar.

Do not create:

* Home
* About
* Contact
* Login

Instead, place **four tiny handwritten text links** in the upper-right corner:

```text
Tonight

Playlists

Saved

About
```

Styling:

* Handwritten
* Understated
* Slightly faded
* Almost invisible

---

# Floating Music Player

Place the player at the bottom center.

Keep it floating above the illustration.

Width:

* 450 px

Height:

* 90 px

Style:

* Frosted glass
* Rounded corners
* Slight transparency

Controls:

```text
◀◀

▶

▶▶

♡

🔁

☾
```

Progress bar:

```text
02:14 ●────────── 05:12
```

Song information:

```text
O Re Piya

Rahat Fateh Ali Khan
```

Do not use album art.

Do not use bright buttons.

---

# Interactive Whiteboard

The whiteboard should become navigation.

Each section should be clickable.

Transform:

```text
Startup Idea → Discover

App Flow → Collections

Features → Genres

To-Do → Recently Played

MVP Launch → Favorites
```

Interaction:

On hover:

* Slight brightness increase
* Small handwritten underline
* No scaling

---

# Playlist Drawer

Add a small cassette-inspired drawer.

Position:

Right side.

Default state:

Collapsed.

Expanded state:

```text
Tonight's Queue

01. Kun Faya Kun

02. O Re Piya

03. Iktara

04. Agar Tum Saath Ho

05. Phir Le Aya Dil
```

Do not allow it to occupy more than **25% of the screen width.**

---

# Window Interactions

Make the window interactive.

Mouse movement should create:

* Slight rain movement
* Water ripple distortion
* Reflection shifts

Movement should be extremely subtle.

Maximum movement:

```text
2–3 pixels
```

No dramatic parallax.

---

# Desk Lamp

Use the lamp as a theme switch.

Interaction:

```text
Lamp ON → Warm Mode

Lamp OFF → Dark Mode
```

The animation should affect only:

* Lamp brightness
* Window lighting
* Whiteboard visibility

Do not invert the entire color palette.

---

Interaction:

Time:


Clicking the clock should open a hidden overlay:

```text

```

---

# Animations

Keep every animation slow.

Use:

* Fade
* Opacity
* Light movement

Avoid:

* Bounce
* Zoom
* Elastic motion
* Large transitions

Animation duration:

```text
200–500 ms
```

---

# Color Palette

```text
Warm Amber

Dark Brown

Dusty Yellow

Muted Gray

Soft Black

Desaturated Blue
```

Avoid:

```text
Pure White

Neon Green

Bright Red

Electric Blue

Cyberpunk Purple
```

---

# Empty Space

This is the most important rule.

**Do not overcrowd the interface.**

Maintain:

* 80% illustration
* 20% interface

If a component covers an important storytelling element, remove it.

Negative space is part of the design.

---

# Final Emotional Test

After opening the website, the user should think:

> I'm listening to music.

Five seconds later:

> Someone is still awake.

Thirty seconds later:

> Maybe that's me.

And one minute later:

> I'll listen to one more song before I sleep.
